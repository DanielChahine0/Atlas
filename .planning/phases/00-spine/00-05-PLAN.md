<objective>
Stand up the DLQ-sink Worker — a tiny, single-concern Worker (its own `wrangler.jsonc`) that consumes the `atlas-wire-dlq` dead-letter Queue and converts every exhausted-retry message into a durable, observable incident: an `audit_log` row in D1 plus a Flagger P2/P3 incident event back onto the Wire. This is the back half of SPINE-05's "never silent loss" guarantee (the front half — provisioning `atlas-wire-dlq` and Steward's `dead_letter_queue` wiring — lands in 00-01 + 00-04).

Purpose: Pillar 5 (idempotent + observable) requires that every notable failure reaches Flagger. Steward's consumer (00-04) routes exhausted messages to `atlas-wire-dlq`; without a consumer on that DLQ, dead events drop silently and counters go stale with no signal (RESEARCH Pitfall 5). This Worker is the mandatory sink that makes a dead event loud instead of lost. Flagger's full agent ships in Phase 2 as a reader/router over `run_log`/`audit_log` and the incident Wire events — this Phase-0 sink writes exactly the substrate (audit row + incident event) Flagger will later score.
Output: A deployable `apps/dlq-sink` Worker consuming `atlas-wire-dlq`, emitting Flagger incidents + audit rows, with a failure-path test proving an exhausted message becomes a P2/P3 incident + audit row and is never silently buffered.
</objective>
